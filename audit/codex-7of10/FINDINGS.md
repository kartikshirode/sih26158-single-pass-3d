## Phase 2: visible faults and scale

**Seam metric.** Pair geometry edges shared by two OBJ triangles. A seam has different UV *coordinates* on its two sides, at tolerance 1e-6; an interior edge has matching coordinates. Sample atlas RGB 5% of triangle altitude inward from the shared edge on each side. Take the mean absolute channel difference, then divide the seam mean by the interior mean. Sample at most 16,000 edges of each class with seed 7. This is a colour discontinuity measure, not a perceptual score. B1: 24.503 vs 5.573 RGB levels, ratio **4.397**; B2: 11.510 vs 6.157, **1.869**; B3: 4.920 vs 1.589, **3.096**; B5v: 5.825 vs 2.874, **2.027**. It ranks B1's visible patchwork above B3's. B3/B5v have constant synthetic lighting yet nonzero seams, so exposure changes cannot explain all of them. B1's per-image gains of 0.92-1.22 from RUNLOG changed little visually; view selection, projection disagreement, blur and lighting remain plausible. This OBJ carries atlas UVs but no per-face source-image identity, so a defensible adjacent patch to keyframe exposure or blur attribution needs a new TextureMesh source-map export. Do not claim exposure alone as the cause.

**Why levelling fails.** RUNLOG Q1, TX1 and TX2 show CPU with both seam levels 9.125 dB/SSIM 0.0115, CUDA with decimation an access violation `0xC0000005` after 46.7 s, and CUDA without decimation 9.117 dB/0.0161 with 10.8% dark atlas pixels. Q1's TextureMesh log completes with 202,921 faces and 11,508 patches but lacks solver diagnostics. OpenMVS 2.4 `SceneTexture.cpp` applies global sparse colour adjustment, then local Poisson border blending, with solver convergence guarded by `ASSERT` in parts of the code. A failed or ill-conditioned solve propagating invalid/dark values is a hypothesis, not proven from these logs. Cheapest discriminating test: one B1 dense-half run each with global only and local only, preserve PNG atlases, log minimum/percentiles and solver status in a diagnostic build before another combined test. RUNLOG's older global-only/local-only results were black too, so a simple flag toggle is unlikely to fix it; the new reason is to isolate solver failure, not retest quality blindly. [OpenMVS 2.4 source](https://github.com/cdcseacave/openMVS/blob/v2.4.0/libs/MVS/SceneTexture.cpp).

**Holes.** B1/B2/B3/B5v OBJ files have 176/106/234/239 topology boundary edges, among 215,120/41,660/382,358/402,279 textured faces. They are mostly closed meshes, so the visible orange gaps are largely *faces without an assigned photo*: atlas samples near OpenMVS's empty colour appear on 7,213/65/3,498/4,443 face centroids, or 3.35%/0.16%/0.91%/1.10%. A face count is not an area or held-out pixel count. B1 held-out coverage 98.79% and B2 72.40% come from prior full-size renders, but those misses were not pixel-classified in this CPU audit. A precise split into never seen, grazing, fusion-filtered and spurious-face-removed requires projected per-view visibility and depth maps, which the saved final runs no longer retain. RUNLOG Q10/Q11 show lowering `remove-spurious` made holes worse; TX3 `close-holes 300` dropped B1 full PSNR to 15.98 from 22.79 dB, so filling all holes is rejected.

**Far field.** In B1, 0-2/2-4/4-8/8-16 model-unit camera distance bins hold 33.58/55.02/11.04/**0.36%** of a 300,000-stride dense-cloud sample; corresponding median view angles are 27.2/59.5/72.5/**78.0 degrees** and median face areas are 0.000964/0.000684/0.000672/**0.001205 model-unit squared**. The distant area has scarce density and near-grazing observations. S1's `overlay_crop_trbl=[0.37,0.08,0,0]` means top 37% plus bottom 8%, leaving 1920x595 keyframes from 1920x1080: the top crop alone removes about **400 rows or 768,000 pixels per source frame**, in **284 of 284 analyzed horizon frames**. It removes the sky and some potential far-field content; the exact portion of houses removed was not segmented. A sky mask on uncropped frames is worth a measured test, but more source pixels do not create parallax.

**No-pitch metric.** Removing `gb_pitch` only and rerunning S5 gives B3 12.559 m median and 1.14% within 1 m, B5v 1.368 m and 10.39%, while GNSS camera RMS stays near 0.126/0.144 m. Camera fit can conceal a wrong ground surface. Synthetic SRT says 24 mm but true focal is 1066 px. A naive full-frame diagonal 35 mm conversion at 1920x1080 implies about 1222 px, 14.6% too high; the effective scale needed here is 0.872. Approximate saved-depth replays at 1222 px, without pitch, leave B3 **6.806 m median, 1.10% within 1 m**, B5v **7.017 m, 1.08%**. They are negative evidence, not end-to-end reconstructions. Real DJI modes have model-specific video crops; the synthetic SRT's 24 mm/1066 px pairing cannot calibrate them. [DJI Air 2S](https://www.dji.com/support/product/air-2s), [Mavic 3 specifications](https://www.dji.com/mavic-3-pro/specs). B1 has a horizon in every analyzed frame, but its row provides one constraint, `y_h-c_y approximately f*tan(pitch)`, for two unknowns. Horizon plus independently known attitude might help; alone it does not fix focal. B3/B5v SRT `rel_alt` is about 110 m, while the synthetic truth ground lies around -5 m in the SRT take-off frame. A real take-off ground elevation and reliably identified ground plane are missing, so using `rel_alt` as camera-to-ground distance would insert an unknown offset; no honest independent accuracy replay can be assigned to that lead. A next-run controlled synthetic replay must test ground selection and take-off elevation without reading truth into the estimate.

**B2 coverage.** The saved final held-out result has 72.40% quarter-size coverage and the RUNLOG full-size result is about 71.97%. The first held-out view covers 38.14%, the second 45.29%, the last 45.00%, while middle views reach about 94%. Its camera span is only 7.42 by 2.66 by 8.59 model units against a median cloud distance of 78.07 units, p90 95.55. This is a pan across distant objects with weak translation baseline. The older 97.12% coverage used a wrong focal/shape, so recovering that number by reverting focal would fail the geometry bar. A visibility and depth test must identify whether missing edge-of-pan pixels lack any supporting source view, or merely failed PatchMatch. Only the latter is recoverable by densify settings.
