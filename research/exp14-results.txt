------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
CONCLUSION (EXP-14)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Two independent rulers agree: the Kolu model is 5.3-5.8x TOO SMALL
  (central 5.5x). Every absolute length it reports is short by that factor.

  1. The plausibility band that accepted this scale (6.4-11.4 m altitude) was the
     defect. A 10.6 m camera height with a 67 deg lens sees ~14 m of ground; the
     keyframes show a four-lane highway, an ecoduct and its verges. The band
     checked internal consistency, not reality, and passed a ~5.5x error.
  2. Scale-free results are untouched: the 14x sampling/information ratio, the
     1.8-3.6x MVS gain, reprojection error in px, every percentage.
  3. A single global correction constant is wrong in principle. The factor is a
     per-run model output; each clip needs its own ruler or its own GNSS.
  4. A 2x correction would leave Kolu measurements 2.7-2.9x short.
