Reconstruct a clip

Upload one continuous drone pass. The same pipeline the rest of this site was built with runs it: admissibility, keyframe selection, camera poses, then photometric densification. Every stage below reports what it actually did, and the timings are measured rather than estimated. A usable model appears about an hour before the final one, because densification is roughly three quarters of the wall clock.

One continuous pass, up to 10 minutes and 600 MB. Sixty keyframes are selected however long the clip is. A run takes roughly 70 minutes end to end; a usable preview arrives at about 20. Results expire, and uploads never appear in the gallery.
