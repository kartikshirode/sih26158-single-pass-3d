A free T4 is 14× faster than 8 vCPU, which turns a 5×-over-budget workload into one that fits — at 11.5 GB, so any 16 GB card will do. That settles the deployment question: one modest GPU is enough.

What this run does not show

Accuracy. The GPS residual was 46 m and the cloud floats ~80 m above the track. Two reasons, neither of which touches the timing result: aukerman is a survey grid, not a single pass — baselines average 64 m with a 265 m maximum and the track criss-crosses, which breaks the assumption behind our single-pass georeferencing fit. And there is no ground truth here anyway: with no ground control points and no reference model, that residual measures agreement with a noisy sensor, not accuracy. Not an accuracy number

Getting a real accuracy figure on real imagery needs a single-pass dataset, ideally with RTK, plus a LiDAR or multi-pass reference to score against.

Reading the two runs together

Where this leaves the build

  • Speed is answered. 600 keyframes fits the budget on a single 16 GB GPU, measured on the real model rather than estimated.
  • Georeferencing is proven on known ground and untested on real ground. Run 1 hits 0.098 m because the scene's truth is known and the flight is a genuine single pass. Run 2 could not test it, because the dataset is a grid.
  • The accuracy claim still depends on RTK. Consumer GNSS gives 2.26 m even with everything else correct — the sensor, not the algorithm, is the limit.
